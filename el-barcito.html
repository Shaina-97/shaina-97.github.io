Project

El Barcito

Digital Marketing Strategy

Helping a local Spanish tapas bar strengthen its digital presence and turn social media attention into restaurant visits.

My Role

What I Worked On

I worked across the research, strategy and planning stages of the project, focusing on how El Barcito could strengthen its digital presence, improve engagement and encourage restaurant visits.

Digital Marketing Research

Researched digital marketing approaches relevant to restaurants and identified opportunities to strengthen El Barcito's online presence.

Social Media Analysis

Reviewed the restaurant's existing social media presence to understand its content, communication style and areas for improvement.

Competitor Analysis

Looked at competing restaurants and their digital activities to identify useful ideas, gaps and opportunities for differentiation.

SWOT Analysis

Analysed El Barcito's strengths, weaknesses, opportunities and threats to support the development of the marketing strategy.

Target Audience Research

Considered the audience the restaurant wanted to reach and how their interests and behaviour could shape the content strategy.

Social Media Strategy

Developed ideas for using social media more strategically to increase awareness, engagement and potential restaurant visits.

Content Strategy

Planned different types of content that could communicate the food, atmosphere and experience of El Barcito.

Instagram Optimisation

Suggested ways to make the Instagram presence clearer, more engaging and better connected to customer actions.

Content Calendar Planning

Planned how content could be organised consistently across different formats and campaigns.

Reel & Story Concepts

Developed ideas for Reels and Stories designed to showcase the restaurant and encourage interaction.

Campaign Ideas

Created campaign concepts aimed at increasing weekday traffic and giving customers more reasons to visit El Barcito.

UGC & Community Engagement

Developed ideas for encouraging customers to share their experiences and become part of the restaurant's online community.

KPIs & Implementation Planning

Considered relevant marketing KPIs and how the proposed strategy could be implemented and evaluated.

Content System

Content System

The strategy focused on creating a more structured social media presence and guiding people from discovering the restaurant to taking action.

What I Learned

What I Learned

This project taught me how to connect creative content with business objectives. Rather than treating social media as only a visual channel, I learned to consider the full customer journey — from discovering a restaurant, to engaging with its content, to making a reservation.
